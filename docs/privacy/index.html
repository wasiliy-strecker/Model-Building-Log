Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

ModellbauLog processes and stores your projects and build steps on your own device. No account, cloud synchronization, advertising or analytics operated by the app.

This Privacy Policy applies to the ModellbauLog mobile app. It explains which data the app handles on your device and when data can leave your device.

“Locally” means processing and storage directly on your own device, where you use the app.

Responsible: Wasiliy Strecker, AppFabrik AI, Ludwigstrasse 23, 86152 Augsburg, Germany.

Privacy and legal contact: contact@appfabrik-ai.de. User support: support@appfabrik-ai.de.

Data handled by the app on your device

  • Project name, model type, manufacturer and material.
  • Build steps with a step number, optional title, date, time, time zone information and notes, for example on paints, parts and work steps.
  • Captured or selected photos in their saved order and technical integrity hashes.
  • Reminder settings and local form drafts.
  • Encrypted backup files created by the user, including all saved build steps and their photos.

Processing on your device and purposes

ModellbauLog processes projects, build steps, photos, notes, reminders and backups locally on your device. There is no user account, cloud synchronization, server AI, advertising or analytics operated by the app. App content is not transmitted to AppFabrik AI or an AppFabrik AI server.

The data is used to document model building projects and their history, compare photos, schedule personal reminders and create encrypted backups. The current position is the chronologically latest build step, regardless of its number. Editing updates the relevant entry. No separate correction history is kept.

The Store variant has neither the internet permission nor the permission to access network state. Its own network connections are therefore blocked. The standalone Dev preview uses the same restriction. Dev Debug retains internet permission for a local connection to the Metro development tool.

Camera and photos

The camera opens only after a corresponding user action. Gallery content is selected through the system photo picker. The app receives access to the selected files. A build step can be saved without a photo or with multiple photos. Photos can be opened, reordered, replaced, removed and compared side by side. ModellbauLog does not use text recognition.

After capture or selection, the app creates a JPEG photo for its private storage, with a maximum longest edge of 1920 pixels and quality level 88. Capture and location metadata are not retained. The source file itself is not changed. It is not archived in ModellbauLog as an unchanged camera original. A replaced or removed photo remains stored only while another entry or draft references it.

SHA-256 hashes refer to the files stored by the app. They help detect subsequent file changes and are neither an official timestamp nor a guarantee of evidentiary integrity.

Device features and permissions

Depending on the feature used, the app accesses the camera, photo picker or file picker for backups. Notifications, alarms and boot events are used exclusively to schedule project reminders chosen by the user and restore their scheduling after a device restart. Android permissions determine whether these features are available. The project name, model type and latest build step may appear in notifications and, depending on your device settings, on the lock screen.

Camera, save-location and sharing actions are initiated by the user. Older Android versions may require storage permissions for these actions. Reminders can be disabled in the app and in Android settings.

Export, sharing and recipients

Data leaves the private app storage when the user saves an encrypted backup at a chosen location or passes it to another app or service through the system share dialog. The user chooses the destination and recipient. This may include an external cloud storage provider. From that point, the selected storage or communication provider's privacy terms also apply.

ModellbauLog does not sell data or share app content for advertising, analytics or profiling.

Security

The database, photos and drafts are kept in private app storage protected by the operating system. Automatic Android cloud backups and device transfers are excluded for this data through Android backup settings. Dev and Store use independent storage areas.

Backups created manually in the app are encrypted with AES-256-GCM and a password chosen by the user. Key derivation uses PBKDF2-SHA256 with 600000 iterations. The app does not permanently store this password. Without the password, restoration is not possible.

Backups include saved projects, all build steps, notes, reminder settings and all associated photos. Unsaved form drafts are not included in the backup. Existing local drafts are preserved when restoring.

Retention

Your projects and build steps remain in private app storage on your device until you delete them, remove the app data through device settings or uninstall the app. ModellbauLog does not operate a server that additionally retains this app content. Editing a build step replaces its previous details.

Backups saved outside the app remain at the chosen location until you delete them there. This also applies to files shared with other apps or people.

Deletion

  • Deleting a build step removes its data. Photos are removed once no saved entry or draft references them.
  • Deleting a project removes its build steps, drafts and reminders. Photos that are no longer needed are then cleaned up.
  • Files saved or shared outside the app must be deleted separately at their respective locations.

Because ModellbauLog has no account or server storage, no account deletion or server-side deletion request is necessary.
